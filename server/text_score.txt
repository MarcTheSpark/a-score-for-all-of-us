The ground beneath us has learned our weight, our shadows have lengthened, shortened, and begun to forget us.
What if we’ve built our hope on an illusion, a sound that only echoes back to mock us?
Listen!
What if he is delayed, stuck, struck, or done for?
I am in the trees. In the stars. In the air. In the water.
Leaves turn without hands. Winds repeat names they never remembered.
Days folded into day, We have been here. 
Will he arrive? 
He will come because we believe. 
Belief is our food.
We have eaten nothing else.
I teach the rope to hesitate, a breath extends.
Winds repeat names they never remembered.
What are we waiting for, the one who never shows up?
Our shadows have lengthened, shortened, and begun to forget us.
The ground beneath us has learned our weight.
He will arrive.
